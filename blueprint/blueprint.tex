\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
This document collects the natural-language statements and detailed proofs that accompany the Lean~4 formalization of Theorem~1.1 of J.~J.~Manfredi, A.~Sobral, E.~V.~Teixeira and J.~M.~Urbano, \emph{Higher-order estimates for $p$-harmonic functions in the plane}.
Six results that the paper cites from the literature (Lewis's polynomial rigidity theorem, the first-order estimates of Lewis and Tolksdorf, the Iwaniec--Manfredi regularity theorem, smoothness away from critical points, interior Schauder estimates for linear divergence-form equations, and local boundedness) are not proved; they are stated precisely and carried as explicit hypotheses (the declarations ending in \texttt{Input}). Everything else is proved.
Each subsection corresponds to the Lean declaration of the same name in the directory \texttt{PHigherOrder/}; cross-references point to the results that a proof uses.
Section~\ref{sec:main} contains the formalized main theorem.
Within each section, items appear in dependency order.
\end{abstract}
