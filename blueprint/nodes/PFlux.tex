\begin{definition}[The $p$-flux]
For $p\in\mathbb{R}$ and $\xi\in\mathrm{Plane}$ set
\[
  A_p(\xi):=\lvert\xi\rvert^{\,p-2}\,\xi ,
\]
where $\lvert\xi\rvert^{\,p-2}$ is the real power of the nonnegative real number
$\lvert\xi\rvert$ with real exponent $p-2$, taken with the usual convention
$0^{a}=0$ for $a\neq0$ and $0^{0}=1$.  Since the scalar $\lvert\xi\rvert^{\,p-2}$ is
multiplied by the vector $\xi$ itself, one has $A_p(0)=0$ for every value of $p$.

This is the flux field of the $p$-Laplace operator: the equation
$-\Delta_pu:=-\operatorname{div}\bigl(\lvert Du\rvert^{p-2}Du\bigr)=0$ of the paper
reads $-\operatorname{div}A_p(Du)=0$.
\end{definition}
