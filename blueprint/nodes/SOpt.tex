\begin{definition}[The Iwaniec--Manfredi optimal order $s(p)$]
For $p\in\mathbb{R}$ set
\[
  s(p):=\frac16\left(7+\frac1{p-1}+\sqrt{1+\frac{14}{p-1}+\frac1{(p-1)^2}}\right),
\]
where $\sqrt{\,\cdot\,}$ denotes the nonnegative square root of a nonnegative real number
(and is taken to be $0$ on negative arguments, a case that does not occur for $1<p<2$).

This is the total order of optimal qualitative regularity for planar $p$-harmonic functions
determined by Iwaniec and Manfredi (\emph{Regularity of $p$-harmonic functions on the plane},
Rev. Mat. Iberoamericana \textbf{5} (1989), 1--19): their theorem gives local
$C^{m,\alpha}$ regularity with $m\in\mathbb{N}$, $0<\alpha\le1$ and $m+\alpha=s(p)$.  For
$1<p<2$ one has $s(p)>2$, and $s(p)\to\infty$ as $p\downarrow1$.
\end{definition}
